# pizza-place-sales-analysis

Exploratory data analysis of a fictitious pizza place's full year of sales data (2015), 
covering order-level and item-level trends across revenue, demand, and menu performance.

## Overview

This project analyzes four linked datasets — Orders, Order Details, Pizzas, and Pizza Types 
— to answer core business questions and uncover deeper patterns in customer behavior.

**Tools used:** Python, pandas, matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook

## Key Questions Explored

- Total revenue, orders, and quantity sold
- Peak sales hours and best/worst days of the week
- Top 5 bestselling pizzas (by quantity vs. revenue)
- Monthly sales trends
- Underperforming pizza types
- Category and size performance
- Ingredient frequency across the menu
- Order size distribution and revenue concentration (Pareto analysis)

## Key Findings

- **$817,860.05** in total revenue from **49,574** pizzas across **21,350** orders
- Demand peaks around **lunch (12–1pm)** and **dinner (5–7pm)**
- **Friday** is the strongest sales day; **Sunday** is the weakest
- Revenue is spread fairly evenly across the menu — no single pizza dominates
- **Large** is the dominant pizza size, accounting for ~46% of total revenue
- **Chicken** pizzas are the most revenue-efficient category per recipe
